# Slayerfest

Simulating academia by writing Buffy papers.

[Slayerfest](https://slayerfest.org/) (in collaboration with [Victoria Ritvo](https://victoriaritvo.com/)) is a fully automated conference made up of fully automated academic labs — staffed entirely by AI bots — dedicated to interpreting private conversations about _Buffy the Vampire Slayer_ and turning them into "publishable" scholarship.

The driving philosophy is: _If there's a problem... add another bot._ The source material: thousands of lines of Buffy-theorizing from a real chat log. The goal: remove the humans who wrote those messages from the interpretive process — entirely. Except as raw data.
